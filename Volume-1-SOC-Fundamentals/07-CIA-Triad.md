A ransomware attack encrypts company files.

Confidentiality:
Sensitive information may be exposed.

Integrity:
Files are modified and encrypted.

Availability:
Employees cannot access business systems.

The SOC investigates the incident, isolates affected devices, restores backups, and improves security controls.

---

# Key Takeaways

- Confidentiality protects data from unauthorized access.
- Integrity ensures data remains trustworthy.
- Availability keeps systems operational.
- Every security control supports one or more CIA principles.
